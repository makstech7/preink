Privacy policy

PreInk collects as little as it can. This page says what that is, in plain words.

What we collect

Only what is needed to build and deliver your kit:

  • Your studio name and, if you upload one, your logo
  • The services you offer and the country and state where you work
  • The style and colors you choose
  • Your email address, when you check out

What we never collect

PreInk does not collect, see or store anything about your clients. Consent forms, health history forms and photo releases are printed and filled in at your studio. No client names, health details or signatures ever reach us.

Payments

Card payments are handled by Stripe. Your card number goes straight to Stripe and is never seen or stored by PreInk. We receive a record that the payment succeeded, the amount, and the email address you paid with.

How we use your information

  • To build your kit and send it to you
  • To let you sign in by email and download your kit again
  • To answer you when you contact us
  • To send a short series of getting-started emails after you buy, and a reminder if you built a preview and left your email. Every one of these emails has an unsubscribe link.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Companies that help us run PreInk

These companies process information for us, only to provide their service:

  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site and stores your kit files
  • Stripe, which takes payments
  • An email delivery service, which sends your download link
  • GoHighLevel, which sends the getting-started emails
  • Anthropic, which provides the AI that suggests wording for non-legal parts of your kit, such as your price menu and scripts

How long we keep it

The PDF files we generate for you are deleted automatically 30 days after they are made. You can sign in and generate them again. We keep a record of your order for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Cookies and tracking

PreInk does not use advertising cookies or sell data to ad networks. While you build a preview, what you type is kept in your own browser so it survives a page refresh. It is not sent to us until you check out.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to support@getpreink.com from the email address you used to buy, and we will do it and confirm when it is done.

Age

PreInk is for adults running or starting a business. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date below and tell existing customers by email.

Contact

Questions about your privacy: support@getpreink.com.

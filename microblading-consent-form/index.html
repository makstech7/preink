What it covers

The parts of a brow consent form.

A consent form records that your client understood the procedure before you started. These are the parts PreInk’s brow procedure consent includes, in the order the client meets them.

  1. Client details

    Name, date of birth, phone and email, so the signed form can be matched to the client record.

  2. Statements the client initials

    Short statements with an initial box beside each one: that permanent makeup is a form of cosmetic tattooing and cannot be wiped away, that results differ from person to person, and that color softens as the skin heals and usually needs a follow-up visit.

  3. Risks and possible reactions

    What can happen during and after the procedure, set out so the client reads each one rather than skimming a paragraph.

  4. The client’s responsibilities

    What the client agrees to do before the appointment and while the brows heal.

  5. Space for state requirements

    Where your state’s required statements go. In a state PreInk has verified, they are filled in. Everywhere else the form is marked as the general version and tells you what to confirm locally.

  6. Signatures and dates

    One line for the client and one for you, each with its own date.

Two forms, not one

Keep health questions on their own page.

Many free templates squeeze a few health questions into the bottom of the consent form. Splitting them works better: the client fills in a health history first, you read it, and only then do you both sign the consent.

A PreInk kit includes a one-page client health history with check boxes for the conditions to ask about, room for regular medications, and a space for you to note that you reviewed it. A photo and video release is a third, separate form, so a client can say yes to the procedure and no to appearing on your Instagram.

Your clients fill these in on paper at your studio. PreInk never sees or stores their names, answers or signatures.

Techniques

Microblading, powder, ombre and combo brows.

The brow procedure consent is written for any brow technique, so every brow client signs the same form. If you also offer lip blush or eyeliner, your kit adds a separate consent form for each, because the risks and healing are different.

Each consent form comes with a matching pre-care sheet and aftercare card, so what the client is told before the appointment, agrees to on the day and takes home all line up.

Before you use it

Check these four things.

Rules for permanent makeup are set by states, and in some places by the county or city. No template can cover every one of them, so check before your first client signs.

  • Your license or registration. Ask your state or local health department what you need to perform microblading where you work.
  • Who can sign. Confirm the minimum age for a client where you work, and whether anyone younger may be treated with a parent’s consent.
  • How long to keep records. Some places set a minimum period for keeping signed forms. Store them somewhere private and secure.
  • Wording your state requires. If your state is not yet verified in PreInk, your form says so on the page. Add anything your health department asks for.

Questions

About brow consent forms.

Do I need a separate consent form for powder, ombre or combo brows?

Not in a PreInk kit. The brow procedure consent is written for any brow technique, so microblading, powder, ombre and combo clients all sign the same form. Lips and eyeliner each get their own consent form, because the risks and healing are different.

Can I use a consent form from another state or another artist?

You can start from one, but rules for permanent makeup differ by state, county and city, and they change. Check what your state or local health department requires before you use any consent form with clients, including one from PreInk.

How long should I keep signed consent forms?

That depends on where you work. Some states and counties set a minimum period for keeping client records. Ask your state or local health department, and keep signed forms somewhere private and secure.

Can clients sign the form on a tablet?

PreInk kits are print-ready PDF files, made to be printed and signed by hand. Digital signing is not included.

Is this consent form legal advice?

No. It is a template, and every page says so. If you are unsure whether a form covers what your state requires, ask your state or local health department or a lawyer.

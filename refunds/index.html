Refund policy

A kit is a digital product delivered straight away, so this is how refunds work.

Look before you pay

You can look through every page of your kit, in your own style, before you pay. Please use the preview to check that it is what you want.

When we refund

Write to us within 14 days of buying and we will refund you in full if:

  • Your kit did not arrive and we could not get it to you
  • The files will not open or print and we could not fix them
  • The kit you received is materially different from the preview you were shown

If something small is wrong, such as a typing mistake in your studio name, tell us and we will correct the kit and send it again at no charge.

How to ask

Email support@getpreink.com from the address you used to buy and tell us what went wrong. We reply within two business days.

What happens when we refund

The money goes back to the card you paid with. Your bank decides how quickly it appears, usually within 5 to 10 business days. Your download link is switched off at the same time, and the documents may no longer be used.

Card disputes

If there is a problem, please write to us first. We can usually fix it faster than a dispute with your bank. A kit that is under dispute has its download link switched off until the dispute is settled.

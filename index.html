State rules

Straight answers about state requirements.

Permanent makeup is regulated differently in every state, and in some places by the county or city instead. No template can promise to cover all of that, so here is exactly what PreInk does.

  • States are added one at a time. Only after the rules have been traced to official government sources and checked by a person.
  • If your state has been added, your forms include the state-specific statements found in those sources.
  • If it has not been added yet, you get a clearly labeled general version that tells you what to confirm with your state or local health department.
  • Every form is a template, not legal advice, and says so on the page.

Questions

Before you start.

Is PreInk legal advice?

No. PreInk gives you templates. Every form says so on the page. Rules for permanent makeup differ by state, county and city, so confirm what applies to you with your state or local health department, or with a lawyer, before you use a form with clients.

Does the kit cover my state?

You can use PreInk in any US state. States are added one at a time, only after their rules have been traced to official sources and checked by a person. If your state has been added, your forms include the state-specific statements found in those sources. If it has not, you get a clearly labeled general version that tells you what to confirm locally.

I don’t have a logo. Can I still use it?

Yes. Type your studio name, choose a lettering style and a color, and PreInk sets it as a text logo on every page. If you already have a logo, upload it as a PNG, JPG or WebP file.

What do I get after I pay?

Print-ready PDF files of every document in your kit, with your studio name, logo and chosen style. They are sent to your email with a private download link, and you can sign in by email to download them again.

Is it a subscription?

No. It is one payment of $47. There is nothing to cancel.

Does PreInk see my clients’ information?

No. Your clients fill in the printed forms at your studio. PreInk only knows what you enter to build the kit: your studio name, your logo, the services you offer, where you work and your email address.

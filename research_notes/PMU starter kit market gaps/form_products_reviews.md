# Reviews, ratings and complaints: PMU form templates and business kits/guides

Research date: 2026-11-17. Method: web search plus page fetches. Etsy listing pages returned HTTP 403 and tinadavies.com returned HTTP 429 to the fetch tool, so Etsy data comes from search-result snippets and structured data, not from the full listing pages. Treat Etsy numbers as point-in-time snapshots that may be stale. No Reddit threads came up in three searches.

## Q1. Best-selling PMU consent / client form bundles: prices, contents, formats, ratings, sales

### Takeaway
Etsy dominates this market. Canva-editable PMU/microblading form bundles are very cheap, typically about $2 to $10 on sale, with "mega" bundles near $20 to $31. A few listings show hundreds of sales, but most have only a handful of reviews. Off Etsy, prices run from about $6 (an indie Word file) to $150 (a California "attorney reviewed" single waiver). I found no PMU form listings on Creative Market, Gumroad, Payhip or Stan Store.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy search results put PMU consent bundle prices at roughly $1.66 to $31.50. The most common Canva PMU consent bundle sells for about $3.00 to $3.93 on sale, and many listings show "marked down from" prices (search snapshot, 2026). Sources: [Etsy market: pmu consent](https://www.etsy.com/market/pmu_consent), [Etsy market: permanent makeup consent forms](https://www.etsy.com/market/permanent_makeup_consent_forms)
- Typical bundle contents: client consent, consultation/intake with medical history, treatment record, photo/video release, patch test form, cancellation policy, liability waiver, and aftercare cards. One listing is a "10X PMU Super Pack" in Canva. Another is a 9-page Canva template that comes with a video on how to edit it. Sources: [Etsy listing 1466553712, Microblading Forms Bundle](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1466553712/microblading-consent-forms-microblading), [Etsy listing 1105402336](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1105402336/microblading-forms-client-consent-intake), [Etsy listing 1651828220, PMU Forms Bundle incl. patch test and treatment record](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1651828220/permanent-makeup-forms-bundle-printable)
- Several listings are sold in multiple formats. One consent and release form comes in Word, Canva and PDF. Source: [Etsy listing 1750225936](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1750225936/pmu-and-microblading-consent-release)
- One Etsy "Permanent Makeup Consent Form, Editable Word & PDF" sells for $9.99. Source: [Etsy listing via search](https://www.etsy.com/mx/listing/1461732362)
- Sales counts seen in search snippets: (849) for one bundle and (888) for one microblading consent set. These are listing-level counts. Source: [Etsy market: microblading forms](https://www.etsy.com/market/microblading_forms)
- The highest-volume microblading consent result showed a 4.7 rating across 3,699 reviews in its structured data. This is probably the shop-wide count, not the listing's; I could not verify which. Source: [Etsy market: microblading forms](https://www.etsy.com/market/microblading_forms)
- Many PMU bundles have very few reviews, for example one bundle with 2 reviews and one consultation form with 1. Sources: [Etsy listing 1651828220](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1651828220/permanent-makeup-forms-bundle-printable), [Etsy listing 1794596374](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1794596374/microblading-consultation-form-editable)
- Other niche listings: a pink PMU pigment removal consent set, "fully editable" ([Etsy 1229706758](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1229706758/pink-pmu-eyebrow-pigment-removal-consent)), and an "Editable PMU Consent Form Template, Microblading & Aftercare (Canva)" ([Etsy 1332625107](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1332625107/editable-pmu-consent-form-template)).
- Outer Beauty PMU (US, Minnesota) sells its own shop product: a $6 editable Word consent and intake form. It runs 7 pages and covers client info, medical history, photo consent, late/reschedule policy, risks, aftercare and acknowledgments, plus a treatment-notes page (color, needle, machine). No reviews are shown. Source: [Outer Beauty PMU](https://www.outerbeautypmu.com/shop/p/permanent-makeup-intake-consent-form-template-with-aftercare-easy-editable-pmu-word-document-microblading-lip-blushing-eyeliner-tattoo)
- Seduire Essentials (US, California) sells a $150 Semi-Permanent Makeup Waiver & Release Form. It is one template delivered as an editable Canva link with fill-in fields, notes on optional provisions and video instructions, and unlimited printing is allowed. It covers procedure, risks, pre/post care, medical history, medications, prior PMU, emergency contact, informed consent and optional marketing photo consent. No reviews are visible. Source: [Seduire Essentials](https://www.seduiressentials.com/product-page/semi-permanent-makeup-waiver-release-form)
- Hannce (UK) PMU Business Bundle: £47 ("Todays Price"). It includes the forms (consultation, procedure information, treatment consent agreement, medical history, appointment record including a "Colour Boost" record), a sensitivity patch test procedure, and a Policy Pack of five business policies. The licence covers personal business use only. A separate Educator Licence is needed to put the materials in student kits. UK product. Sources: [Hannce SamCart page via search](https://hannce.samcart.com/products/pmu-business-bundle) (fetch returned 403, so details come from search snippets)
- Free alternatives compete on price: hosted PMU consent and consultation templates from Paperform, Jotform, Typeform, forms.app, Formnx and GoCanvas. Sources: [Paperform](https://paperform.co/templates/permanent-makeup-consent-form/), [Typeform](https://www.typeform.com/templates/permanent-makeup-consent-form), [forms.app](https://forms.app/en/templates/permanent-makeup-consent-form), [Jotform](https://www.jotform.com/products/appointments/templates/salon-forms/makeup-forms)

### Inferences
- Forms are a race-to-the-bottom commodity on Etsy (about $3), and free hosted templates exist alongside them. The one premium signal is a legal-review claim, which is how Seduire charges $150.
- Sales counts are small even on the "best sellers" (hundreds), which suggests this is a long-tail market with no dominant brand.
- Trainers fold forms into kits or guides (Tina Davies' Ink to Income, Hannce) more often than they sell them on their own.

### Gaps
- Could not open Etsy listing pages (403), so I have no full review text, Star Seller status, exact current prices or exact review counts per listing.
- I found no PMU form products on Creative Market, Gumroad, Payhip or Stan Store. The searches returned only Etsy results, so this is an absence in search, not proof none exist.

## Q2. What buyers praise and complain about

### Takeaway
The visible praise centers on how easy the forms are to edit in Canva and how complete the bundle is. Visible complaints are rare because review volume is low and sellers moderate, but the one surfaced 1-star complaint was about printing. I found no direct evidence for several hypothesized complaints (typos, generic wording, UK wording, no state specificity, Canva lock-in); they are inferred from listing structure only.

### Cited Findings
- Praise (Etsy reviews in search snippets): forms "super easy to edit and make it my own", and "Very easy to edit in Canva and customize". Source: [Etsy market: microblading forms](https://www.etsy.com/market/microblading_forms), [Etsy 1332625107](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1332625107/editable-pmu-consent-form-template)
- Praise for completeness: an esthetician bundle described as "everything and more" the buyer wanted. Source: [Etsy esthetician consent forms](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1559027316/esthetician-consent-forms-forms-editable)
- Complaint: a 1-star review on the high-volume microblading consent result said the buyer could not print the forms as they were. Source: [Etsy market: microblading forms](https://www.etsy.com/market/microblading_forms)
- Canva dependence: most listings are built for Canva, and one notes it can be edited in the free version of Canva. Source: [Etsy esthetician consent forms](https://www.etsy.com/market/esthetician_forms)
- Some sellers charge extra for custom branding. Source: [Etsy facial consent listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1119373013/facial-consent-form-consultation)

### Inferences
- Buyers who expect a ready-to-print PDF, or who lack Canva skills, are likely to be friction points. The printing complaint and the "video tutorial included" selling point both point that way.
- Generic multi-state wording is the norm. No Etsy listing seen claimed state specificity.

### Gaps
- I could not verify complaints about typos, generic or non-state-specific wording, UK spelling or wording sold to US buyers, missing forms, or low design quality. No review text was reachable, and I found no Reddit or forum threads.

## Q3. PMU business guides and courses: price, contents, reviews, criticisms

### Takeaway
Business products range from a cheap downloadable guide (Tina Davies' Ink to Income) to mentorships costing thousands of dollars (PMU Launch at about $5,600, Brows by Linnie at $15,000). Independent reviews exist mainly for the high-ticket coaching programs, and they are mixed, with refund and value complaints. For PhiAcademy, Brow Daddy and The Brow Lady, I could not find business-course pricing or reviews.

### Cited Findings
- Tina Davies' "Ink to Income: The Business Setup Guide by Carla Ricciardone" is a downloadable guide. Its listed contents include client form templates, a list of state licensing boards and a PMU startup checklist. The product has an options selector, and I could not read the price (site rate-limited). Sources: [tinadavies.com product](https://tinadavies.com/products/ink-to-income-the-business-setup-guide-by-carla-ricciardone), [UK store version](https://uk.tinadavies.com/products/ink-to-income-the-business-setup-guide-by-carla-ricciardone)
- PMU Launch (US, Kyle Koschel), a business and marketing coaching program, has mixed Trustpilot reviews. One reviewer says a refund promise was made and the service was not worth the $5,600 paid plus required ad spend. Another wrote they wished they had "gone with my gut and said no". The new owner replied that the business was rebuilt after an acquisition. Source: [Trustpilot PMU Launch](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/pmulaunch.com)
- Third-party reviews of PMU Launch report complaints that the price was too steep and about the refund policy, and list "no price transparency" and no refund policy as cons. Both reviews are affiliate-style. Sources: [Side Hustles Database](https://sidehustlesdatabase.com/pmu-launch-review/), [IPPEI](https://ippei.com/pmu-launch/)
- PMU Society (a business program) answered a negative Trustpilot review by saying the reviewer had not run the program "as designed". Source: [Trustpilot PMU Society](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/pmusociety.com?page=2)
- Brows by Linnie's business-focused PMU program costs $15,000 (or $2,500/month for 6 months) and covers pricing courses and tiered packages (2025 listing). Source: [Schedulista Brows by Linnie](https://www.schedulista.com/schedule/browsbylinnie/b6otx-1vfnr)
- Omnia Academy sells a "PMU Business Setup Course" on Teachable. No price was visible. Source: [Omnia Academy](https://omnia-academy.teachable.com/courses/2031669)
- Hannce's PMU Business Bundle (UK, £47) is forms plus a policy pack, not a course. Source: [Hannce](https://hannce.samcart.com/products/pmu-business-bundle)

### Inferences
- The market splits into two tiers: cheap templates and guides (under $50 to $100) and expensive coaching ($2.5k to $15k). The coaching tier draws refund and value disputes. A mid-priced, concrete "business setup kit" for US artists looks thinly served.

### Gaps
- No business-course pricing or reviews found for PhiAcademy, Brow Daddy or The Brow Lady blueprint. Searches returned nothing relevant.
- No price or review count for Ink to Income (tinadavies.com returned 429).

## Q4. Lawyer-reviewed or state-specific claims and disclaimer wording

### Takeaway
A few US indie sellers claim attorney review tied to one state (California, Minnesota). Even these pair the claim with strong "not legal advice, check your state" disclaimers. I saw no Etsy listing claiming legal review. Generic sellers and form builders tell buyers to consult a lawyer.

### Cited Findings
- Seduire Essentials (CA) says on different parts of its pages that the templates were "reviewed by attorneys", "reviewed by California licensed attorneys" (FAQ) and "carefully reviewed by an attorney" with the PMU/beauty industry in mind. It also says it is not a law firm, that none of its staff are licensed attorneys, that no attorney-client relationship exists, that templates are "as-is" with no warranty, and that buyers must indemnify it and keep up with changes in the law. Source: [Seduire Essentials](https://www.seduiressentials.com/product-page/semi-permanent-makeup-waiver-release-form)
- Outer Beauty PMU (MN): "We had a Minnesota attorney review this form", but it is "highly recommended to check your state laws and insurance requirements." Source: [Outer Beauty PMU](https://www.outerbeautypmu.com/shop/p/permanent-makeup-intake-consent-form-template-with-aftercare-easy-editable-pmu-word-document-microblading-lip-blushing-eyeliner-tattoo)
- Paperform's free template tells users to consult a lawyer and replace its provisions with their own. Source: [Paperform](https://paperform.co/templates/permanent-makeup-consent-form/)
- Ink to Income includes a list of state licensing boards, which addresses state differences by pointing to the boards rather than through state-specific forms. Source: [tinadavies.com](https://tinadavies.com/products/ink-to-income-the-business-setup-guide-by-carla-ricciardone)

### Inferences
- "Reviewed by an attorney in state X" works as a premium marketing claim (about 25x the Etsy price), but none of the vendors stand behind it legally. A product that is honestly state-aware for all 50 states appears absent.

### Gaps
- I found no product claiming coverage of all 50 states or a per-state version. I could not independently verify any attorney-review claim.

## Q5. Refund and chargeback complaints for digital form products

### Takeaway
Sellers of digital forms almost all use "all sales final" or very limited refund terms. I found no documented chargeback complaints for form products. Refund disputes show up mainly with high-ticket coaching programs.

### Cited Findings
- Seduire Essentials: "All sales are final", with no refunds or exchanges because the product is electronic. Source: [Seduire Essentials](https://www.seduiressentials.com/product-page/semi-permanent-makeup-waiver-release-form)
- Etsy form sellers: one states digital downloads are not eligible for refunds. Another offers no refunds but will fix reported problems. A third allows a 7-day refund window and asks the buyer to explain why. Sources: [Etsy esthetician forms market](https://www.etsy.com/market/esthetician_forms), [Etsy facial consent listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1119373013/facial-consent-form-consultation), [Etsy esthetician consent listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1717051718/esthetician-consent-template)
- Hannce: the licence bars resale or sharing, and educators need a separate licence. Source: [Hannce](https://hannce.samcart.com/products/pmu-business-bundle)
- Coaching: PMU Launch reviewer alleges a broken refund promise on a $5,600 program. Source: [Trustpilot PMU Launch](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/pmulaunch.com)

### Inferences
- At a $3 to $10 price, buyers rarely bother to dispute. Dissatisfaction more likely shows up as quietly not using the forms, which also helps explain the low review counts.

### Gaps
- No chargeback data or specific refund complaints were found for PMU form templates. No Reddit or Facebook group threads were reachable through search.
